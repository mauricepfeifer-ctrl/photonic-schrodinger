# Mission Control Center

Welcome to the Mission Control Center for your AI Empire.
This system acts as the "Top Rail" (Oberste Schiene), orchestrating your agents, services, and revenue streams.

## Quick Start

1. **Check Status**: `./scripts/mc_status.sh`
2. **View Logs**: `./scripts/mc_logs.sh <service_name>`
3. **Restart Service**: `./scripts/mc_restart.sh <service_name>`
4. **Dashboard**: Open `dashboard/index.html` in your browser.

## Structure

- `artifacts/`: Raw inventory data and logs.
- `registry/`: Service definitions (`services.yaml`).
- `scripts/`: Control scripts.
- `docs/`: Documentation and plans.
- `dashboard/`: Visual interface.
